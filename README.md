# AKSH — Mental Wellness Center

### A Safe Space For Every Mind

AKSH is a mental wellness brand built around one simple idea:

> Every mind deserves a safe space.

## Stage 0 — Arrival Experience

The first experience of the AKSH website is a cinematic, time-aware entry screen.

It includes:

- Automatic local date and day
- Time-based greeting
- Time-based cinematic background video
- Morning experience
- Afternoon experience
- Evening experience
- Night experience
- AKSH branding
- AKSH icon
- Dynamic emotional messaging
- Sound control
- Skip control
- Begin Your Journey CTA
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Reduced-motion accessibility support
- Safe video loading and fallback behaviour

AKSH is being developed as a premium, emotional, human-centered digital experience rather than a conventional counselling website.

The website is being built sequentially, beginning with the Stage 0 arrival experience and expanding into the complete AKSH ecosystem.
