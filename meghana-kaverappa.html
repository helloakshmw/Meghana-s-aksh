The person behind AKSH

AKSH began with a belief — that every mind deserves a space where it can be understood.

Meghana Kaverappa is the Founder and Chief Psychologist of AKSH Mental Wellness. Her work forms the human foundation from which AKSH is beginning to grow.

A human beginning

Before AKSH becomes a world, it begins with one person listening.

The early journey of AKSH is intentionally personal. Today, Meghana leads the psychological work herself, creating a foundation built on professional care, trust and human connection.

A way of seeing

You don't always need to have the answers. Sometimes you need a safe place to find them.

AKSH is being built around the understanding that seeking support is not a sign that something is wrong with you. It can simply be a decision to understand yourself a little better.

There is a person behind every story. And every person deserves to be heard.

As AKSH grows, the vision is to bring together psychologists, counsellors, educators, researchers and people who believe in building a healthier relationship with mental wellbeing.

Come as you are.

Your journey doesn't have to begin perfectly. It just has to begin.
