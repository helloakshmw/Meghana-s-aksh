AKSH · Privacy

Privacy.

Your trust matters. AKSH is committed to treating personal information with care, respect and appropriate confidentiality.

About this policy

This page explains, at a general level, how AKSH may handle information when you visit or use this website.

Important: This website is being developed progressively. Before the website begins collecting personal information, the final privacy policy should be reviewed and approved for AKSH's actual services, technology providers, forms, booking systems and applicable legal requirements.

Information you may provide

Depending on the services eventually enabled on this website, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Information needed to respond to an enquiry.
  • Information needed to arrange a session or appointment.
  • Information you voluntarily provide through communication tools.

AKSH should only request information that is reasonably necessary for the particular purpose.

Website information

Like many websites, technical information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or other infrastructure providers when those services are enabled.

The exact services used by AKSH will be documented in the final privacy policy before launch.

Confidentiality

Mental-wellness conversations can be sensitive. Website privacy, professional confidentiality and any applicable legal or professional obligations are separate matters and should not be treated as interchangeable.

The final AKSH service policies will clearly explain the confidentiality arrangements applicable to each service.

AI and digital communication

If AKSH introduces AI-assisted communication, visitors will be clearly informed when they are interacting with an AI system.

AI should not be represented as a human psychologist or as a substitute for professional care.

Your choices

Depending on the information collected and the service involved, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or other handling of your personal information.

The final implementation will provide the appropriate contact method for privacy-related requests.

Updates

This privacy page may be updated as AKSH's services, technology and legal requirements develop.

The effective date of the final policy will be displayed here before public launch.
