Sometimes it helps to say it somewhere.

AKSH AI is designed as a gentle starting point for reflection.

You can describe what you are feeling, what has been occupying your mind, or simply write what you cannot quite explain yet.

The purpose is not to diagnose you. It is to help you slow down, organise your thoughts and find a possible next step.
